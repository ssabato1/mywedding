Where Europe's venues use castles and lakes as a backdrop, venues in New York and Sydney use the city. From a rooftop the skyline, the river and the harbour are all in view at once. Drawing on venue guides from Joy and Easy Weddings, here are the conditions of rooftop and city venues. (As of November 2026)

## New York: the skyline as decoration

The venues in Joy's [guide to rooftop wedding venues in NYC](https://withjoy.com/blog/rooftop-wedding-venues-nyc) are not large.

| Venue | Borough | Listed capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Press Lounge | Manhattan | Up to 150 |
| 620 Loft & Garden | Manhattan | Up to 200 |
| The William Vale | Brooklyn | Up to 150 |
| Wythe Hotel | Brooklyn | Up to 120 |
| Westlight | Brooklyn | Up to 100 |

Between 100 and 200 guests. A rooftop is not wide, but the city is visible from every seat. The same guide lists what a rooftop wedding needs: a rain backup, heaters, and a running order timed for golden hour, the hour before sunset.

## Sydney: terraces facing the harbour

Easy Weddings' [guide to rooftop wedding venues in Sydney](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/articles/rooftop-wedding-venues-sydney/) features venues that face the harbour. Dockside in Darling Harbour is listed at up to 550 seated, Cohibar at about 200 and View by Sydney at about 350, while the rooftop terrace at the Bristol Arms is listed at 80 seated or 200 standing.

Note that the same space carries separate seated and standing numbers. Many Sydney rooftops are planned for a standing cocktail reception more than for a seated meal.

![A canapé reception on the rooftop — welcome drinks and finger food (a wedding venue rooftop on the Han River, Seoul)](/images/canape-reception-01.jpg)

## Four conditions of a rooftop wedding

1. **Rain**: a rooftop has no roof. The indoor alternative and the time of the decision should be written into the contract.
2. **Wind**: it is stronger at height than on the ground. Veils, flowers, programmes and candles are all affected.
3. **Temperature**: it falls quickly after sunset. Heaters, blankets and somewhere to rest indoors are needed.
4. **Sound**: city rooftops often have noise rules. Check how late music may be played.

## What to check when applying this in Korea

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Seated and standing capacity | The same rooftop holds very different numbers depending on how you serve |
| Indoor space if it rains | It has to be in the same building for guests to move |
| Heaters, shade and blankets | Standard equipment for spring and autumn evenings |
| Hours for sound | This sets the end of the party |
| Lifts and access | Older guests, luggage and catering all have to reach the roof |
| Whether the slot is three hours or more | Golden hour and the night view both need time |

The rooftop garden of one wedding venue on the Han River in Seoul, for example, looks onto the river, the bridge and the city lights on every side, and if it rains the wedding moves to an indoor hall in the same building. Whichever rooftop you are considering, ask these six questions first.

## Summary

- Rooftop venues in New York and Sydney are often for 100 to 200 guests, with the city view doing the work of decoration.
- Seated and standing capacities differ, so decide how you will serve first.
- Check rain, wind, temperature and sound before signing.
