Of six months of planning, the shortest and busiest stretch is the last 48 hours. This is that stretch, from the evening before to the settlement the day after, in order. The times are those one wedding venue in Seoul actually gives; they vary a little by venue, so replace them with the times in your contract and from your D-2 check.

## D-1 evening — one bag, three calls

**Put everything in one bag.** If you start looking on the morning, it is too late.
- Rings (in their case), two printed copies of the vows (one for the MC)
- Marriage registration papers (if filing on the day), ID
- Cash and envelopes (for the MC, singers and helpers), cards
- Basic medicine, spare tights, insoles and plasters, needle and thread, safety pins
- Chargers, a power bank, the sound files on USB (with a cloud link)
- Lipstick and powder for touch-ups, sweat pads (in summer)

**Three calls.**
1. To the MC and singers: arrival time (usually 50 minutes before) and the final running order
2. To both sets of parents: arrival time (90 minutes before) and parking
3. To the photographer and videographer: arrival time and where shooting starts (make-up studio or venue)

**If overseas family has arrived**, share a short dinner. Keep it brief and simply repeat tomorrow's shuttle time and the dress code.

**Before bed.** For an outdoor wedding, look at the forecast once more and confirm with the venue when the decision to move indoors is made. If it is already decided, stop looking.

## The day — before the ceremony

| Time (to ceremony) | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −4:00 | Bride | Make-up and hair begin (at a studio; −3:00 if in the venue's make-up room) |
| −3:00 | Groom | Make-up and dressing; check rings and vows |
| −2:00 | Photographers | Begin with the bride getting ready |
| −1:30 | Couple and both sets of parents | **Arrive at the venue.** Bridal room and parents' room |
| −1:30 | Shuttle | Starts running from the Express Bus Terminal or other pick-up point |
| −1:15 | Venue coordinator | Final rehearsal with the couple (entrance route, microphones, music cues) |
| −1:00 | Reception desk | In place: guest book, envelope box, meal tickets |
| −1:00 | Bride | Bridal room photographs begin (greeting guests until −0:40) |
| −0:50 | MC, singers, musicians | Arrive; sound check |
| −0:30 | Parents | Greet guests in the lobby |
| −0:10 | Couple | Bridal room closes; wait for the entrance |
| 0:00 | Everyone | Ceremony begins |

**Decide by D-1**: whether to receive guests in the bridal room (it shortens the time for photographs), and whether group photographs come before or after the ceremony. If before, arrive 30 minutes earlier.

## The day — during the ceremony (in the order the couple chose)

During the ceremony the couple has one job: **not to look at the time.** The MC and venue coordinator run the order. Instead, give three people roles in advance.

- **One friend in charge**: the link between MC, coordinator and photographers. The couple takes no calls on the day.
- **Two at the reception desk**: registration, safekeeping of gifts, meal tickets. They hand the envelope box to the parents afterwards.
- **One guide for overseas guests**: an English-speaking friend who checks the shuttle has arrived, shows guests to tables and explains the menu.

## The day — after the ceremony

| Time | What |
| --- | --- |
| +0:00 | Group photographs (family → friends → colleagues, called by the photographers) |
| +0:20 | Meal and reception begin. The couple go round the tables |
| During the meal | After-party, first dance, toasts (if in your running order) |
| End −0:30 | **First settlement check** with the coordinator: actual meals served (by tickets collected), drinks consumed, extra guests |
| End | Collect the envelope box, guest book, leftover favours and flowers (whether to keep the bouquet). Check the bridal room |
| End +0:30 | Parents leave; last shuttle |

![After the ceremony — the after-party bar (a wedding venue on the Han River in Seoul)](/images/after-party-bar-01.jpg)

## The day after — settlement

Most venues send the settlement the day after the wedding. There are five things to check.

1. **Meals**: actual meals against the guarantee. Guests beyond the allowance (usually 10%) are charged extra; a shortfall is charged at the guarantee.
2. **Drinks**: bottles if charged by consumption, heads if a package.
3. **Extras**: flowers, equipment or extra time added on the day.
4. **Outside items** such as banners and the photo table — check nothing is charged twice.
5. **Confirmation that the balance was received**, with receipts.

Along with the settlement, send thanks to the coordinator, MC, singers and photographers. Original photographs usually arrive two to six weeks later.

## What to check when applying this in Korea

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival times for the couple and parents | They vary by venue. Write the times from your D-2 check into the table above |
| Whether a rehearsal is included on the day | Entrance, microphones and music cues need even fifteen minutes together |
| Whether the first settlement check happens before the end | Checking meals and drinks on site avoids disputes the next day |
| The time of the last shuttle | If the after-party runs long, guests need a way home |
| Whether the bouquet and flowers can be kept | Whether you take the flowers or the venue disposes of them |

For example, Sevit Island and Marina Park on the Han River in Seoul ask the couple and parents to arrive 90 minutes before the ceremony, the reception desk one hour before and the MC and singers 50 minutes before, with a 10% allowance over the guarantee and drinks settled by consumption on the day. Other venues differ only in the numbers, so change the times in this table and put it on the fridge.
