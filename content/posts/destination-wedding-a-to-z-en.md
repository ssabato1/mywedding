A destination wedding is held in a city where neither of you lives. Because most guests have to travel, you are planning several days, not one. Drawing on guides from Joy and THE WED, here is the order of preparation. Costs vary so widely by country and season that this article leaves amounts out.

## Timeline: start 12 to 18 months ahead

The timeline in Joy's [destination wedding guide](https://withjoy.com/blog/how-to-plan-a-destination-wedding-the-complete-guide/) runs about half a year ahead of an ordinary wedding.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 12–18 months | Set budget and guest count, book the venue, send save-the-dates, launch a wedding website |
| 9–12 months | Book local vendors, negotiate a hotel room block, send invitations, begin legal paperwork |
| 6 months | Confirm vendors, remind guests about rooms, book your own travel, plan the welcome dinner and brunch |
| 3 months | Send the detailed itinerary, check documents, arrange local transport, send RSVP reminders |
| 1 month | Give final numbers, send the last logistics email |

THE WED's [etiquette guide](https://thewed.com/magazine/destination-wedding-etiquette) puts save-the-dates at 6 to 10 months and formal invitations at about 3 months before. Guests need time to arrange leave, passports and a budget.

## Who pays for what

The two guides agree.

- **Guests**: their own flights and accommodation.
- **The couple**: the ceremony and reception, plus hosted events such as a welcome dinner or next-day brunch.
- **Optional**: some couples help immediate family with travel costs.

Since guests are spending a great deal to come, the hosts prepare the experience of the stay: a city tour, a family dinner, a small welcome gift in the hotel room.

## Guest numbers and attendance

According to Joy's guide, a destination wedding typically has 20 to 80 guests, fewer than the 100 to 150 of an ordinary wedding. Places that need two connections reduce attendance, and older guests, families with small children and guests on a tight travel budget may not be able to come.

THE WED also suggests holding a small reception after you return for those who could not attend.

## Separate the legal marriage from the ceremony

To marry legally in another country you must follow that country's paperwork and procedure. Joy's guide explains that many couples register their marriage at home first and hold a symbolic ceremony at the destination, because notice periods and residency requirements differ from country to country.

International couples holding their ceremony in Korea can do the same: register the marriage according to each country's procedure, and choose the date and place of the ceremony freely. Confirm the document requirements directly with your embassy and the local district office.

## Rooms and information

- **Room block**: negotiate with the hotel 9 to 12 months ahead. According to Joy, most hotels ask for a minimum of 10 rooms, and the guest booking deadline is set 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding.
- **Wedding website**: directions, hotels, dress code, schedule and common questions in one place.
- **Language**: email is the most reliable channel for overseas guests. Prepare a separate guide in English.
- **The weekend**: welcome dinner, then ceremony and reception, then brunch the next day.

## What to check when Korea is the destination

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Travel from the airport and terminals to the venue | Shuttle, public transport, coach parking |
| Hotels near the venue | Distance from the hotel where you hold a room block |
| English-language hosting and guest information | MC, programme and menu cards |
| Halal, vegetarian and allergy alternatives | The more overseas guests, the more this must be standard |
| Whether the slot is three hours or more | Guests who travelled far need time with you |
| Space for the dinner before and the gathering after | Whether the whole weekend can be planned in one place |
| How to consult from abroad | Email, messenger, video meeting |

One wedding venue on the Han River in Seoul, for example, is close to the Express Bus Terminal, offers English-language hosting and alternative menus, and holds consultations by video meeting for couples living abroad. If Seoul is your destination, the table above works as a list of questions for the site visit.

## Summary

- Start 12 to 18 months ahead and send save-the-dates early.
- By custom, guests cover flights and accommodation, and the couple covers the ceremony and hosted events.
- Many couples marry legally at home and hold the ceremony at the destination.
- A room block, a website and information in English are the three supports of guest care.
