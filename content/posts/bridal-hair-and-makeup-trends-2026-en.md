Until a few years ago the test of bridal make-up was that it would not move. In 2026 the test is that it looks like skin. Using THE WED's round-up of 2026 bridal beauty trends, here are the directions in make-up and hair, and how to choose for the venue. (As of December 2026)

## Make-up: skin comes first

These are the directions in THE WED's [Major Bridal Beauty Trends for 2026](https://thewed.com/magazine/major-bridal-beauty-trends-for-2026).

- **Warm glow skin**: skin with a soft champagne light, using cream highlighter, honey bronzer and soft metallic eyeshadow.
- **Skin-first minimalism**: the article presents the approach of celebrity make-up artist Nina Park as a trend in itself. Concealer only where needed, lifted brows, cream blush, nude lips and a diffused line at the eye.
- **An accented lip**: a light face with all the emphasis on the lips. Deep red for the ceremony, rosy nude with gloss for a softer look.
- **Soft gothic glam**: blackberry or smoky rose lips and deeper eyes, presented as a match for vintage dresses and evening ceremonies.

All four share a thin base. Nothing is heavily covered, and there is only one point of emphasis.

![A bride in a long veil — the closer the photograph, the more the skin shows](/images/bride-veil-subcut.jpg)

## Hair: sculpted and neat, or short

The same article names three directions in hair.

- **Sculpted Hollywood waves**: glossy S-shaped waves, sleeker than full curls.
- **Editorial updos**: updos shaped by twists, knots and folds of the hair itself, with little ornament.
- **The bridal bob**: bobs and short cuts, finished with a mini veil, a sculptural pin or a bow.

The biggest change is that growing your hair is no longer assumed.

## Nails: small and precise

For nails the article shows crystals placed like pinpoints, French manicures reworked with pearl edges or fine lines, and metallic art in gold leaf and chrome. Since the hand with the ring is photographed up close, the measure is the tone of the dress and the ring.

![Lace on white — the finer the texture of the dress, the lighter the make-up](/images/sposabella-dress-01.jpg)

## Choosing for the venue and the hour

| Setting | Make-up | Hair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows and daylight | A thin base and cream textures. Daylight shows heavy make-up as it is | Waves with tidy edges, or a low bun |
| Outdoor garden | Setting where it is needed for wear, and sun protection | An updo or half-up style that holds in wind |
| Large hall with LED lighting | One step more definition in contour and lips, to read from a distance | An updo with a clear shape, or Hollywood waves |
| Evening and night view | An accented lip or deeper eyes come alive under lighting | A glossy finish |

![Garden and deck outdoors — plan for wind and sun (a wedding venue on the Han River, Seoul)](/images/sevit-garden-01.jpg)

## What to check when applying this in Korea

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Whether the trial is at the same time of day as the ceremony | Colours look different in day and evening light |
| The colour of the venue's lighting and the direction of its windows | Daylight and artificial light call for a different base |
| Mirror lighting and touch-up space in the bridal room | You need somewhere to retouch during the day |
| A touch-up plan if the wedding runs three hours or more | One retouch between ceremony and reception |
| How the veil or headpiece is fixed | The position changes with the hairstyle |
| Wind and humidity if outdoors | They decide how the hair holds |

## Summary

- Make-up in 2026 centres on a thin base and a single point of emphasis.
- Hair now ranges from sleek waves and updos to short cuts.
- Before any trend, look at the light of the venue and the hour of the ceremony. Have the trial at the same time of day.
