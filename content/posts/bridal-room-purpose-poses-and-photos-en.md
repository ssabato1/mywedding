On the wedding day, the place where a bride spends the longest is not the hall but the bridal room. For an hour or so, in her dress and waiting for the doors to open, the room is a dressing room, a reception room and a small photo studio at once. Here is how the room is used, how to look after your feelings in it and how to photograph it well. (As of November 2026)

![Examples of bridal room flowers — the mood of the room changes with pink, white or violet (Anniversary Flower)](/images/bridal-room-flower-board.jpg)

## What the bridal room is for

The Western bridal suite is mainly a place to have hair and make-up done and to get dressed. Guests do not come in; only the bride, her family and her bridesmaids are there. The Korean bridal room is closer to a room where the bride, already dressed, receives her guests. While the groom and both sets of parents greet people in the lobby, the bride sits in the bridal room, meets the friends and relatives who come to see her and has photographs taken with them.

So three uses overlap in a Korean bridal room.

- **Finishing touches**: the veil, the train and the bouquet are arranged one last time, and make-up is retouched just before the entrance.
- **Receiving guests**: once the ceremony begins there is little chance of a long conversation with the bride. The bridal room is almost the only time guests meet her up close.
- **Photographs**: pictures with guests, portraits of the bride alone and family photographs all happen here.

International couples should explain this custom to overseas guests in advance. Guests from cultures where the bride is not seen before the ceremony often hesitate at the door.

## How it feels in the room

A bride in the bridal room feels excitement and nerves together. Every time the door opens a welcome face comes in, while part of her mind keeps running through the order of the entrance, her steps and her parents. Gladness, trembling and a dazed sense that none of it is quite real come by turns, and that is natural.

The Knot suggests several [ways to stay relaxed on the wedding day](https://www.theknot.com/content/ways-to-stay-relaxed-wedding-day).

- **Eat a little at a time**: an empty stomach makes nerves worse and can lead to dizziness. Keep bite-sized snacks and water with a straw in the room.
- **Leave room in the schedule**: ten or fifteen spare minutes give you time to breathe or to be quiet on your own.
- **Calm music**: play music you like, softly, during hair and make-up and while you wait.
- **Send questions elsewhere**: agree that the planner or coordinator takes the day's questions.

One more thing: before guests arrive, make five minutes to be in the room alone or with the one person you are most at ease with. Those few minutes of looking at yourself in the dress, slowly, are almost the only time in the day when a bride can attend to her own heart. If tears come, there is no need to hold them back. That is what the retouch is for.

## Sitting and posing

Most bridal room photographs are taken seated. A few points keep you comfortable for the hour and neat in the pictures.

- **Back and shoulders**: do not sink into the sofa; sit on the front half with your back straight, shoulders down and neck long.
- **Angle**: rather than facing the camera square on, turn the body slightly and turn only the face. The shoulder line and the silhouette of the dress show better.
- **Hands and bouquet**: hold the bouquet low, below the navel. Raised to the chest, it lifts the shoulders and hides the bodice. Relax the hands and keep the fingers lightly together.
- **The train**: spread it in a curve in front of you, and have your attendant rearrange it whenever a guest sits beside you.
- **Gaze**: you need not look at the camera in every frame. Looking at the window, the bouquet or the person beside you often gives the more natural picture.
- **Laughter**: photographers say that asking people to laugh brings a more genuine expression than asking them to smile. [SLR Lounge](https://www.slrlounge.com/bride-getting-ready-photography/) also advises that in group pictures people should stand close enough to touch shoulders or arms and lean slightly towards the bride, which makes the photograph look closer.

## Getting good photographs in the bridal room

- **Light**: [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/content/getting-ready-wedding-photos-tips) recommends replacing harsh overhead lighting with softer lamps or opening the shades for natural light. In a room with a window, the best seat has the window to one side. Direct midday sun washes out skin, so filter it once through a sheer curtain.
- **Flowers and colour**: the same article suggests matching the room's flowers and colours to the look of the wedding. The colour of the flowers becomes the background of the photographs. With a white dress, white and green read as clean, while pink or violet adds warmth to the face.
- **Tidiness**: bags, hangers, water bottles and shopping bags stay in the photographs. Decide on a corner for belongings that the camera does not face.
- **Time**: allow a margin in case hair and make-up run late, and take the portraits of the bride alone and the family pictures before guests arrive in numbers.
- **Shots to agree in advance**: full length from front and back, a close-up of the face, the detail of the back of the dress, holding hands with your parents, and the profile just before the doors open.

## What to check when applying this in Korea

| Item | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Distance and route to the entrance of the hall, and whether it is easy to walk in the dress |
| Hours | How long before the ceremony the room is available, and whether it overlaps with the previous wedding |
| Light | Whether there is a window; colour temperature and dimming of the lighting |
| Flowers | What the standard styling includes, and whether colours and varieties can be changed |
| Seating | Whether guests can sit beside the bride's sofa for photographs |
| Storage and restroom | A place for belongings, and a restroom that can be used in the dress |
| Overseas guests | Whether the invitation or guide explains that guests may visit the bride in the bridal room |

At one wedding venue on the Han River in Seoul, the flowers of the bridal room can be chosen by colour, and generous intervals between ceremonies mean there is no rush in the bridal room either. Whichever venue you choose, the most reliable check is to sit in the bridal room before signing and take one picture on your phone from that seat.

## Summary

The bridal room is where preparation is finished, guests are received and photographs are made. A bride feels excitement and nerves there together, so arrange snacks, spare time and a few minutes alone in advance. A straight back with the body slightly turned, a bouquet held low, soft side light and a tidy background are enough for beautiful bridal room photographs.
