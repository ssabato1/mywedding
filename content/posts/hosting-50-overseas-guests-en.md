With 50 guests coming from overseas, a wedding stops being a one-day event and becomes three days of hosting. You cannot show family who flew in a one-hour ceremony and send them home. Based on guides from The Knot and Joy in the US, here are rooms, transport and meals, stage by stage.

## Rooms: block them 9–12 months ahead

Joy's [2026 hotel room block guide](https://withjoy.com/blog/how-to-book-a-hotel-room-block-for-your-wedding-2026-guide/) recommends contacting two or three hotels **9–12 months** before the wedding — 12–14 months in peak season (May to October) or popular areas. The usual number of rooms is 30–40% of the guest count, rising to 70–80% for a destination wedding where most guests travel.

There are two kinds of block.
- **Courtesy block**: the rate is held and the hotel takes back unsold rooms. No financial exposure, which suits a smaller wedding.
- **Contracted block**: rooms and rate are fixed, and the couple pays a share (attrition) of rooms left unfilled. It suits a large wedding.

Set the guest booking deadline four to six weeks before the wedding and send reminders **at least twice** from six to eight weeks before the deadline. Joy notes that many guests mean to book and forget.

Applying this in Korea: hotels in Seoul respond better to a "group booking" enquiry than to the term "room block". Suggest two hotels near the venue at different price points and perhaps one guesthouse, and guests can choose easily. Some hotels provide a shuttle when 20 rooms or more are booked together, so do ask.

## Transport: airport to hotel, hotel to venue

The Knot's [guide to out-of-town guests](https://www.theknot.com/content/how-to-make-out-of-town-guests-feel-at-home) recommends that, since most guests arrive by air, **the couple arrange transport to and from the venue**. Informal vehicles such as a school bus or trolley are fine, and for guests who prefer their own schedule, put taxi company numbers on the wedding website.

What to prepare in Korea:
- **Incheon Airport → hotel**: set out the airport limousine routes and stops, the range of taxi fares and the options for a late-night arrival, in English. A line on installing Kakao T helps.
- **Hotel → venue**: a shuttle timetable. Two runs, 90 and 60 minutes before the ceremony, are enough. Time the return to the end of the party.
- **Coach**: if guests are in one hotel, a single 45-seat coach is simplest. Check first that the venue has coach parking.

## Meals: not just the wedding day

Overseas guests usually arrive the day before and leave the day after. The couple needs to think about three meals, not just the reception.

1. **The evening before — welcome dinner or welcome drinks**: The Knot recommends a light gathering the evening before for out-of-town guests not invited to the rehearsal dinner. It often becomes the first meeting of the two families.
2. **The day — the reception**: ask in the invitation reply whether any guest avoids alcohol or particular meats for religious or health reasons. Confirm that the venue prepares halal, vegetarian and allergy alternatives **at no extra cost**.
3. **The day after — brunch or a Seoul tour**: not obligatory, but in overseas surveys 37% of couples hold at least one extra event such as a welcome party or next-day brunch.

## Information: one website, one welcome bag

The Knot recommends making the wedding website **the centre of all information** and putting its link on the save-the-date. It should hold the schedule, addresses, transport, accommodation, dress code, and personal recommendations such as the couple's favourite local restaurants and the place they met.

On arrival, hand over a **welcome bag**: the weekend schedule, shuttle times, the venue address, water and snacks, and a transit card for guests new to Korea is plenty.

## What to check when applying this in Korea

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Is the venue easy to reach from the airport or express bus terminal? | Overseas guests do not know the way. The shuttle pick-up must be simple |
| Is there coach parking? | One 45-seat coach is easier than twenty taxis |
| Are halal, vegetarian and alcohol-free options on the standard menu? | So you do not hear "not possible" on the day |
| Is there a small dining space on site for the evening before? | A welcome dinner at the venue saves guests one journey |
| Does the venue produce English guides and menu cards? | The couple does not have to translate everything |

For example, Sevit Island on the Han River in Seoul runs a shuttle from the Express Bus Terminal, has parking for 900 cars with coach parking, and keeps halal and vegetarian alternatives as standard. At a venue with these conditions, the transport and meals of 50 overseas guests are largely out of the couple's hands.
